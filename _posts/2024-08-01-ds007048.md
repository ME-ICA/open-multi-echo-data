14 participants.
12 participants have 1 session with 1 run of the auditory task.
3 participants have 2 sessions- the first has 1 run of auditory task and the second has 4 runs of the task.

There are CO2 regressors in the derivatives, but they're not in the raw BIDS data.
I didn't label this dataset as having "physio" because I don't know if the CO2 regressors can be treated as raw data.

Based on the preprint ([Medina et al., preprint](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.06.29.735344)), I think that the ses-2 data from subjects 01, 02, and 03 is the same as the data for subjects 01, 02, and 03 in ds008070.
