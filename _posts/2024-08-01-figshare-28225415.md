Three simultaneous EEG-fMRI datasets were collected from overlapping groups of healthy participants: eyes-closed resting state (ME-REST), a respiration task (ME-TASK), and a reaction-time task (ME-TASK-CUE).

ME-REST includes 11 participants and 15 scans, ME-TASK includes 6 participants and 9 scans, and ME-TASK-CUE includes 12 participants.

Multi-echo EPI data have 3 echoes (TE = 13.0, 29.4, and 45.7 ms), with TR = 2.1 s and 3 mm isotropic voxels.

The dataset also includes simultaneous EEG, photoplethysmography, and respiration data. Anatomical and functional MRI images are supplied as raw NIfTI files.

The dataset is directly downloadable from Figshare under CC BY 4.0.
