Right-handed healthy participants underwent two experimental sessions on separate days, following 30 minutes of real or sham transcranial static magnetic field stimulation (tSMS) over the right motor cortex.

The dataset includes multi-echo resting-state fMRI, diffusion-weighted imaging, and high-resolution T1w MRI. Phase images are included; version 1.0.1 corrected their scaling to the -pi to pi range.

The 35.2 GB archive is directly downloadable from Zenodo under CC BY 4.0.

The Zenodo landing-page metadata does not report the number of echoes or scanner field strength, so those front-matter fields should be filled after verifying the acquisition metadata in the archive.
