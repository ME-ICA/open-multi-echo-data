The current release contains baseline data from 28 participants: 20 participants with multiple sclerosis and 8 healthy controls.

There are up to 2 resting-state runs per participant. Multi-echo data have 5 echoes (TE = 13.4, 31.1, 48.8, 66.5, and 84.2 ms), with TR = 1.355 s and 2.4 mm isotropic voxels.

The dataset also includes T1w, T2w FLAIR, multi-shell diffusion MRI, AP/PA field maps, and PCASL data.

The dataset is released under CC0.
