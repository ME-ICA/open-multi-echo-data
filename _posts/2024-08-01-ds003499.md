90 participants between 8 and 25 years old performed value-directed memory encoding and retrieval tasks.

Multi-echo data have 4 echoes (TE = 13.60, 30.88, 48.16, and 65.44 ms), with TR = 2 s and multiband acceleration factor 2.

The dataset is released under CC0.
