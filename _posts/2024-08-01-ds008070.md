5 participants with T1w, 4 runs of MBME auditory task.

Multi-echo data have 3 echoes.

There are CO2 regressors in the derivatives, but they're not in the raw BIDS data.
I didn't label this dataset as having "physio" because I don't know if the CO2 regressors can be treated as raw data.

Based on the preprint ([Medina et al., preprint](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.06.29.735344)), I think that the data from subjects 01, 02, and 03 is the same as the ses-2 data for subjects 01, 02, and 03 in ds007048.
