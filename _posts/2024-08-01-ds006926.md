83 participants.
The multi-echo data have magnitude+phase reconstruction and three echoes.
The dataset also includes 10-echo MEGRE data.

There are three tasks: resting-state, a visual oddball, and a visuo-motor task.
The physiological data includes electrocardiography and respiration.

The data were acquired on two 3T Siemens Prisma scanners and scans were acquired with either TR=1800 ms or TR=800 ms.
